Auto-catch

Arm it with the eye button on the view. When it hears a loud noise or sees sudden movement it fires Catch moment by itself. Sound needs the microphone on; for motion keep the phone steady (a tripod is ideal).

Safety

The padlock button on the view blocks every tap so a bump can't stop the recording. Hold the unlock button for about a second to release it.
